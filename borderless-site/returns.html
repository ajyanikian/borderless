Returns & refunds

Food should arrive safely, accurately, and in good condition. If something goes wrong, we want to help.

Change-of-mind returns

Because our olive oil is a food product, we do not accept change-of-mind returns after an order ships, even if the tin is unopened.

This policy does not limit any rights you have under applicable law.

Damaged, missing, or incorrect orders

If a tin arrives damaged, your package is missing an item, or we sent the wrong product, contact hello@borderlesstheory.com as soon as possible, ideally within 7 days of delivery.

Include your order number, a brief description, and photos of the product and packaging if available. We will review the issue and, when appropriate, offer a replacement or refund at no additional cost.

Cancellations before shipping

If you need to cancel, email us promptly. If the order has not shipped, we can generally cancel it and refund your payment. Once shipped, cancellations may not be possible.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Your bank or card issuer controls how quickly the credit appears.

For questions about an order, email hello@borderlesstheory.com.
