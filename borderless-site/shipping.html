Shipping

We ship Palestinian olive oil across the 50 U.S. states. Shipping is calculated from the actual packages needed for your order.

Where we ship

We currently accept orders shipped to addresses in the 50 U.S. states, including Alaska and Hawaii. We do not currently ship to Washington, DC, U.S. territories, APO/FPO/DPO addresses, or international destinations.

A valid address and available carrier quote are required before an order can be placed.

Shipping cost

Shipping is calculated at checkout using available Shippo carrier rates. The cost depends on the destination and how many boxes are required.

Two cans fit into one box; larger orders may be packed across multiple boxes. The final shipping charge is shown before payment.

Processing and delivery

Orders are packed and prepared manually by our small team. Orders typically dispatch within 7–10 business days after an order is placed, excluding weekends and U.S. public holidays. Dispatch means handing the package to the carrier, not delivery to your address.

Transit times vary by carrier and destination; Alaska and Hawaii may take longer. Any delivery estimates shown by a carrier are estimates rather than guarantees.

When a package ships, we will share tracking details when available. If we cannot ship within an applicable promised or legally required timeframe, we will contact you with the option to accept a delay or receive a refund.

Address or delivery problems

Check your address carefully before ordering. If you notice a mistake, email hello@borderlesstheory.com as soon as possible with your order details. We cannot guarantee changes after a parcel has shipped.

If your order arrives damaged, is missing items, or appears lost, please contact us so we can investigate and make things right.
