Privacy

We collect information needed to run the store, fulfill purchases, and respond when you contact us. Here is how that works.

What we collect

When you make a purchase or contact us, we may receive your name, email, shipping address, phone number (if provided), order details, payment status, and the contents of your messages.

Our website and service providers may also process basic technical information such as IP address, browser details, cookies, and security logs needed to operate and protect the site.

How information is used

  • To calculate shipping, process orders, communicate about purchases, and handle customer support.
  • To prevent fraud, secure the site, troubleshoot issues, and meet legal or accounting obligations.
  • To respond to messages or subscription requests you choose to send.

Service providers

Borderless uses Stripe for payments, Shippo and shipping carriers for delivery, and Cloudflare for website hosting and order infrastructure. They may process information needed to provide these services.

Payment details are entered through Stripe. Borderless does not collect or store complete card numbers in its order database.

Email forms on this website

Our current “Subscribe” and “Share my vision” features open your email application. They do not themselves create a subscription in an automated mailing-list database. If you send the email, we receive the information you choose to include.

Cookies, tracking, and your choices

Stripe, Cloudflare, or other essential service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for security and checkout functionality. We have not implemented a separate personalized-advertising tracker in the storefront code reviewed for this draft.

The website does not currently provide a separate response to browser “Do Not Track” signals. You can manage cookies using your browser settings. If our tracking or advertising practices change, this policy should be updated.

Retention and protection

We retain relevant order and communications records as needed for fulfillment, customer service, disputes, bookkeeping, and legal requirements. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee perfect security.

Your choices and questions

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing hello@borderlesstheory.com. Requests are reviewed and handled as required by applicable law; some order records must be retained for legal reasons.

We may update this policy as our operations or legal requirements change. The updated date above identifies the current version.
